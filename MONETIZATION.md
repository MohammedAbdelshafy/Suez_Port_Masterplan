# Suez Port Masterplan Commercialization

## Product
Parametric CAD generation and engineering-report automation for early-stage port/masterplan studies.

## Suitable commercial uses
- concept design and feasibility visualization
- parametric CAD generation
- BOQ/report packaging for preliminary review
- custom templates and training for engineering teams

## Buyer candidates
Maritime consultants, civil/marine engineering firms, port developers, and academic/research teams.

## Commercial boundary
The current project describes itself as a course/project deliverable. Do not imply professional certification, construction readiness, or code compliance without independent review and documented validation.

## Pilot
One customer-defined vessel/port scenario with explicit assumptions, generated DXF/PDF/CSV deliverables, and an engineering review checklist.

## Readiness gate
First verify inputs, formulas, units, reference standards, repeatability, and generated artifact quality. Then build a sanitized demo case and define support scope.
