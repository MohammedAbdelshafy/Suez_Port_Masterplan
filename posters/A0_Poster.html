Objectives

To design a state-of-the-art, parametric deep-water port masterplan at Suez, integrating advanced maritime engineering principles, automated CAD generation, and sustainable infrastructure to support the next generation of global shipping.

Planning-level concept design. Verified against PIANC guidelines.

Site Location & Context

Located at the strategic southern approach to the Suez Canal, Egypt. The site presents unique coastal morphology constraints, bounded by existing maritime traffic corridors to the east and environmental preservation zones.

Coordinate System: Local Grid (E = +X, N = +Y)

Design Methodology & Tooling

Coastal Structures Studio (CSS v1.0)

To manage the complex parametric relationships between vessel dimensions, PIANC guidelines, and spatial constraints, a custom Python-based engineering engine was developed for this project.

Workflow: Vessel Inputs → PIANC Calculations → DXF Generation → BOQ
Advantage: Ensures rigorous mathematical compliance for all geometries.
