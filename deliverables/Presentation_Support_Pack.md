### 3. Examiner Questions & Suggested Answers

**Q1: Why did you choose a 1.5x LOA turning basin instead of 2.0x LOA?**
*Answer:* "A 1.5x LOA basin (375m) is the PIANC standard for modern vessels assisted by tugs in moderate environmental conditions. Since our tug basin is located immediately adjacent to the turning area, tug response time is instantaneous, making the 1.5 multiplier safe and economically efficient."

**Q2: How did you arrive at the -14.1m dredged depth?**
*Answer:* "The depth is derived from the design vessel's maximum draft (dmax) of 12m. I applied a standard PIANC under-keel clearance (UKC) of 15% (1.8m) plus a 0.3m allowance for vessel squat in shallow confined waters. 12 + 1.8 + 0.3 = 14.1m."

**Q3: Explain the breakwater armour sizing.**
*Answer:* "I utilized the Hudson Equation. With a significant wave height (Hs) of 5.2m, specific gravity of rock (Sr) of 2.6, a damage coefficient (Kd) of 4.0 for rough angular rock in two layers, and a slope of 1:1.5, the required median stone weight (W50) evaluates to 7.6 tonnes."

**Q4: How does your masterplan address environmental impact?**
*Answer:* "The layout incorporates a 60,000 square meter landscaped buffer zone. Furthermore, the Liquid Bulk terminal features fully bunded tanks with a 45m spacing to prevent spill propagation, adhering to international maritime safety standards."

**Q5: What was the purpose of your CSS v1.0 tool?**
*Answer:* "CSS v1.0 is an engineering support tool. It does not replace engineering judgment; rather, it automates the translation of PIANC formulas into geometric constraints. This ensured that every CAD line I drew was mathematically backed by the design vessel's specifications."
